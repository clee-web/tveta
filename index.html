Our story

Through learning, we avoid poverty.

AMUAA takes its name from “Kwa Masomo Tuepuke Ufukara”: through learning we avoid poverty. Founded in 2019, we are a private institution in Kisumu focused on youth empowerment and employability.

We bring together students, efficient processes, qualified teachers and quality programmes, and we challenge learners to think independently.

Questions, answered

Before you apply.

Can I study while I work?

Yes. Classes are customised to learners’ needs, with online / e-learning, evening, afternoon, weekend and part-time options.

What documents do I need to apply?

A passport-size photo, your national ID or passport, and academic certificates or result slips. A CV is optional. Upload PDF, JPG or PNG files of up to 8 MB each.

How much will my course cost?

Fees depend on the course and study option. We offer competitive rates and flexible payment plans. Call admissions or email us for the fee for your chosen course.

How long are the courses, and what are the entry requirements?

Duration and entry requirements vary by course. Choose your course in the application and admissions will follow up with the details, or ask an adviser before you apply.

Is AMUAA licensed and accredited?

Yes. AMUAA is licensed and accredited by TVETA and NITA, and is affiliated with KYEOP, KNEC, KSTVET, CDACC and NYOTA.

Where are your branches?

Our CBD branch is at Melona House, and our Riat Market branch is opposite Kisumu International Airport.

What happens after I submit my application?

You’ll see a reference number as soon as your application is received. Admissions then contacts you using the details you provided.
