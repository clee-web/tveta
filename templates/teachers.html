How it works

  1. Add the teacher here and upload their qualifications.
  2. Open the teacher to assign classes.
  3. Give the teacher their username & password (also shown on their profile).
  4. The teacher logs in at /teacher/login and sets a new password.
  5. In the portal they can view students, mark the register by QR scan, and download / upload exam results.

Classes are groups of students by course + academic year + study option. Create them on the Classes page.
