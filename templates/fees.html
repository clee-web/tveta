Registration fee
Varies by course

Each course sets its own registration fee, so editing it on the Academics page changes what that course's students are billed. A student's bill is the registration fee plus the sum of every fee structure for their course and academic year. Balances update automatically whenever a fee is saved or a payment is recorded.
